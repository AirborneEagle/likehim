Like Him — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-08

Like Him is a personal devotional reflection app built around the Christlike Attribute Activity from Preach My Gospel. We collect only what is necessary for the app to work, and we never sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

What we collect

  • An account identifier — a Firebase anonymous user id, or your email address if you create an account.
  • Your optional display name.
  • Your reflections: ratings (1–5) for each statement, optional notes, timestamps, and the attribute you've chosen as your current focus.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under a Firebase project operated by the developer (Tyler Christensen). Access is restricted to your own account by Firestore security rules that disallow any other user from reading or writing your data.

What we share

Nothing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or analytics. Like Him does not integrate any ad networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.

How to delete your data

Open Like Him → Settings → "Erase all my reflections" to remove everything stored for your account. You can also email support@likehim.app and we will delete it manually.

Children

Like Him is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 except as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and adjust the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes acceptance.
