A reflection, not a measurement.

Like Him is built around the Christlike Attribute Activity from chapter 6 of Preach My Gospel. You sit prayerfully with ten attributes — Faith, Hope, Charity, Virtue, Knowledge, Patience, Humility, Diligence, Obedience, and Integrity — and gently rate where you are on the Never → Always scale.

It remembers your reflections. A radar chart shows your most recent one; line charts show how each attribute moves over time. Every scripture reference taps through to the verse on churchofjesuschrist.org.

There are no streaks, no badges, no headlining numbers. The point isn't to beat yesterday. The point is to notice, to be honest, and to keep coming back.

Quiet by design

No gamification. No notifications you didn't ask for. No social feed.

Yours forever

Your reflections sync across devices and stay only with you. We don't share, and we don't sell.

Anonymous-first

Try it without an account. Upgrade later — your reflections come along.
